Commands

👑 Global Owners

Global Owners have access to every PristBot command, including moderation, server management, owner commands, Prist Coins administration, and global commands.

👤 Member Commands
-afk [reason]

Set your AFK status

-invites [@user]

View invite statistics

-invlb

View the invite leaderboard

-info [@user]

View user information

-msgs [@user]

View message statistics

-commands

View your available commands

🪙 Prist Coins Commands
-balance [@user]

View a Prist Coin balance

-daily

Claim your daily reward

-weekly

Claim your weekly reward

-work

Work for Prist Coins

-beg

Beg for Prist Coins

-shop

View the Prist Coins shop

-buy <item>

Buy an item

-inventory

View your inventory

-use <item>

Use an inventory item

-givecoins @user <amount>

Give your coins to another user

-coinflip

Flip a coin

-roll [sides]

-guess <1-10>

Guess a number

-quiz

Answer a quiz for coins

-coinlb

View the coin leaderboard

-cooldown

View your coin cooldowns
